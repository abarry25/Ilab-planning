How to use this comms tool
The idea

One row per platform, one column per week. Every message sits on the day it actually goes out. The top row shows what's running that week — the events your messages can point at.

Four kinds of message
Dated sendGoes out on one day, to whoever you pick. The everyday case.
JourneyA sequence running over weeks, where people join at different points — a weekly digest, an onboarding series.
Entry emailThe welcome for people who joined during one window. Belongs to a journey.
AutomationFires when someone does something rather than on a date, and only while it's switched on.
Reading the board
sticky noteA message on its send date. Click to open it.
solid barA journey running across those weeks. Click to open it.
hatched barAn automation — live, waiting on a trigger.
│ tick on a barWhere one entry cohort ends and the next begins.
⌗ on a noteWhich Slack channels it posts to.
⊣ on a noteWho's eligible — the enrolment cutoff or cohort window.
! on a noteSomething needs a look. The bar at the top of the page says what.
The four dates, and which is which
Sends onThe day it goes out. Every dated message has one.
Cohort opensAn entry email's start. People joining from this date get that welcome, until the next one opens.
Must be in system byA journey message's enrolment cutoff. Join after it and you don't get this one. Leave it empty to send to everyone.
Active from / untilThe window an automation is switched on. Outside it, nothing fires.
Doing things

Hover any week and click + to add a message there. + Journey and + Automation are in the calendar header. The Journeys, automations & channels tab is where you manage lanes, Slack channels and audience segments. Expand all events opens up every busy week at once, or click +N more on a single one.

Getting things in and out
Import events CSVYour Central Intake export from ClickUp. Once loaded, content slots pick from real events instead of free text.
Import planChoose merge to add someone else's plan to yours — nothing is deleted, and matching channels are reused. Replace wipes everything and asks twice.
Download plan CSVOne row per content item, with every date and audience spelled out. Good for sharing or for whoever builds the sends.
Export / import planA full backup of the plan as a file.
The bar at the top

It counts what's in the plan and lists anything that looks wrong — a Slack post with no channel, an automation with no trigger, a cutoff that excludes everyone, a message promoting an event that will already be over. It's advice, not a gate: nothing stops you saving, because sometimes the odd-looking thing is what you meant.

Saving

Changes save themselves and are shared with everyone on the plan — the note under the term selector tells you the current state. If it says local only, the page isn't talking to the server and your edits are staying in this browser.

Ordered by cohort opens. Each covers everyone entering from its date until the next one starts; the first covers everyone from the journey's start. Sends on is the day that email goes out to its cohort — leave it empty and it fires whenever each person joins instead.
Messages in this journey
Each message has two dates. Must be in system by is the enrolment cutoff — someone who joins after it doesn't receive this one. Sends on is the day it goes out. Leave the cutoff empty to send to everyone in the system on the send date.
