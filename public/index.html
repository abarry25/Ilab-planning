How to use this: each row is a program or task pulled from the AY26–27 planning tool — click any name to rename it, use + Add row at the bottom of a section to add a new one, and hover a row to reveal the × to remove it (removals show an Undo for a few seconds). In day view, click individual days; use Collapse to weeks to mark a whole week at once (click again to expand back to days — your day-level marks are preserved either way). Type initials into the small box for who owns it, and use the notes field for cadence or context. Check the box on any row and click Create ClickUp export to generate one line per marked date, ready to edit and download as a CSV for import. Milestones pinned above the grid come from the FAS Registrar's published calendar — double-check exact dates before locking programming around them. Switch the Fall 2026 / Spring 2027 tabs at the top to plan the next term whenever you're ready; each term keeps its own board. Group tags: MACRO = ecosystem events to track, FUNC = an operating rule rather than an event, CORE = flagship i-lab programming, SERVICE = supporting programs and partnerships. Use the Find items from / through bar above the grid to pull everything marked for a single date, or leave both dates filled in for a range (e.g. This week or Next 7 days) to see a whole week's worth grouped day by day — including registrar milestones — across whichever term(s) the dates fall in. In day view, right-click any marked cell to add a start/end time, location, and a comment for that specific date — a small dot marks cells with details, and those values flow straight into the ClickUp export's start/end time and location columns when you build it. Every bucket also carries a rollup strip on its header row: a slim band showing every day that bucket has anything at all going on, so you can read a whole functional area at a glance — collapse a bucket and the strip is still there, which is the fastest way to see, say, all the days Venture Incubation is running. Sub-groups get their own strip too, so the picture simplifies at each level, and the N days chip next to each name counts how many distinct days that level touches. Days with three or more items stacked on them are flagged with a small dot so pile-ups are easy to spot. Click any filled square in a rollup strip to list exactly what's behind it — that view is read-only, so it can't change your marks. If names are getting cut off, drag the thin handle just right of the name field to widen it (double-click to reset) — or drag the handle at the far edge of the left panel to widen the whole names/owner/notes area at once.
